## What Worked Well

- **Structural-enforcement-first.** Every P0 made silent loss *inexpressible at the seam* (min/max composition, `required` exemption basis, coverage gate making `Completed`-over-uncovered unreachable, no-skip-path-survives), not merely tested. Both reviewers verified the property is structural, not behavioral — the strongest possible bar.
- **Author≠implementer non-vacuous locks.** All 12 RED-proven on pre-fix code (several via Docker SQL/ES TestContainers `-m:1`, cp-backup mutate-restore). The independent test seat caught the fixer's blind spots.
- **The integrator gate (verify-before-claiming) earned its keep — 3 distinct catches:** a ruling-crossing (Tests executed a retracted flip), and two incomplete handoffs on `eszc06` (the integration "done" was the integration tier only; the fake-store + the contract were each caught at the gate before committing a red tree).
- **Grounded-challenge discipline.** The `eszc06` saga deep-dive: three grounded challenges (Docker breakage → contract smell → guard-drop-insufficient) converged on the correct EF-style store-owns-increment contract instead of shipping a hazardous one. SA self-corrected pins; the developer code-verified the merge trace.
- **Targeted liveness management.** Worker tail-idles resolved with staged nudges (idle-but-alive sessions revived in seconds); the terminated DocumentationWriter session correctly escalated to the operator for restart rather than a silent stall.

## What Didn't Work

- **STRATEGY phase was skipped.** For an audit / "fix-a-class" sprint, the CEO's class-extinction reframe (10→12 + DoD hardening) landed *mid-sprint* instead of at the STRATEGY gate. **Recommendation below.**
- **F-5/NFR-6 sweep was scoped too narrowly twice.** A contract-changing fix broke pre-existing tests in *sibling* files (the `eszc06` fake-store; the 4 EventSourcing flips) that only full CI caught — the F-5 sweep grepped the lock's file, not every test referencing the changed type.
- **bd tracker flush daemon-lock recurred** under multi-worker contention (`Access is denied` on `beads.base.jsonl`), forcing 5 separate code-then-reconcile commit pairs. Handled (commit-with-note + retry), but it's friction.
- **`git add -A` of a deleted path aborted a multi-path add** (`kitw4i`), briefly committing a non-compiling HEAD; fixed immediately with a completion commit.

   Evidence: 5 broken-behavior tests in sibling files (`eszc06` fake-store + 4 EventSourcing) caught late by full CI. Effort: one extra grep per contract-changing fix, at IMPLEMENT not TEST.

3. **[CONTINUE] Integrator gate: re-run the full guard set on committed mainline; never trust a "done" claim.**
   Evidence: 3 real catches this sprint (ruling-crossing + 2 incomplete handoffs) — each would have shipped a defect or a red tree.

   Hypothesis: the recurring daemon-lock is transient contention that clears on retry; a structural bounded-retry (already a project rule) removes the 5 manual reconcile commits. Measurement: zero standalone `chore(beads): reconcile` commits next multi-worker sprint.

5. **[CONTINUE] Structural-enforcement-first + author≠implementer non-vacuous locks.**
