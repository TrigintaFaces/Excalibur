## Process learnings

### 1. Broadcast-REWAKE cluster hang (NEW, notable, 1× this sprint)

A single `critical`+`ackRequired` fan-out broadcast (msg 44722, 9 recipients — the
IMPLEMENT-complete announcement) REWAKE'd the roster and **3 sessions hung on it**
(TestsDeveloper, ProjectReviewer, DocumentationWriter — all `REWAKE`-without-`START`, heartbeats
frozen 15:01:24). Backend/SA/Platform took the same REWAKE and revived. **Same-sprint mitigation
demonstrated:** the CLOSE completion summary used `high`/no-ack (`wakeCount:0`) and caused no hangs.
Correlation observed between large `critical`+`ack` fan-out broadcasts and session hangs; a burst of
directed wakes to many recipients at once appears to be the trigger, not the message content.

### 2. Non-waking-reply stalls (recurring ~4×, ALREADY COVERED)

Rulings/pins landing via `opcom_reply` (no `@mention`/ack) do not wake the blocked worker → silent
idle-stall on an answer that already exists (SA's `7o4jbq` seam pin, the EraseEvents ruling relay,
the SA-pin-didn't-wake-Backend cases). Already covered by `coordinate-before-parallel-work` clause 5
("make the signal actually WAKE the recipient") + `verify-before-claiming` (dispatched≠received).
Caught each time by the PM's proactive mtime-gated liveness sweep + poll-log `REWAKE`-without-`START`
discriminator.

### 3. Carry-through recovery validated end-to-end

REVIEW_CODE, and DOCS; **author≠reviewer preserved** (Platform declined to review its own 3 beads →
a fresh independent project-reviewer sub-agent did REVIEW_CODE). Validates the `run-autonomous-hive`
clause-4 mechanism + `verify-worker-liveness-before-dispatch` (mtime-gate, three-state hung model,
revival-safe).
