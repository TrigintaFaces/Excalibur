## What Worked Well

1. **GUIDE phase prevented wasted work** — SA identified 3 tasks that were over-scoped or already resolved. Without GUIDE, BD would have spent time on non-existent problems.
2. **Zero review findings** — Both REVIEW_CODE and REVIEW_ARCH approved with 0 findings. The team's alignment on patterns (internal-first, ValidateOnStart, ISP) is paying off.
3. **Duplicate detection** — 5 duplicates identified and closed before/during sprint. Beads backlog hygiene is improving.
4. **Phase auto-advancement** — Mission pipeline flowed smoothly from PLAN through CLOSE with minimal PM intervention (only 2 manual `check_phase` calls needed).

## What Didn't Work

1. **Mission task completion lag** — BD and TD completed their work but didn't always call `opcom_task_update(status: "completed")` promptly, requiring PM to force-advance phases with `check_phase`. This happened in IMPLEMENT and TEST phases.
2. **AOT validation toolchain** — vswhere.exe PATH issue continues to prevent AOT validation locally. This has been waived across multiple sprints.

## Surprises

1. **Sprint was ~40% smaller than planned** — Model B deletion in S832 was more thorough than initially assessed. This is a good problem — it means S832 was well-executed.
2. **62-minute sprint** — One of the fastest sprints in project history. Cleanup sprints with clear scope and no spec work are efficient.

---

## Recommendations

1. **CONTINUE** — GUIDE phase for every sprint. Evidence: Prevented 3 wasted tasks in S833. Cost: ~5 minutes of SA time.

2. **CHANGE** — Workers should call `opcom_task_update(status: "completed")` immediately when finishing, not just post a completion message. Evidence: PM had to force-advance 2 phases. Fix: Add reminder to worker dispatch messages.

3. **CONTINUE** — Duplicate detection during sprint planning. Evidence: 5 duplicates caught, preventing confusion and double-work.

4. **TRY** — Pre-sprint backlog grooming pass that cross-references new issues against recent sprint deletions. Hypothesis: Would have caught bd-upg4a4 (already resolved) before it entered the sprint plan. Measurement: Count of tasks closed-as-resolved in next 3 sprints.

5. **STOP** — Waiving AOT validation indefinitely. Evidence: Waived in S832 and S833. Fix: Create a Beads task to resolve the vswhere.exe PATH issue or update the validation script to handle missing toolchain gracefully.
