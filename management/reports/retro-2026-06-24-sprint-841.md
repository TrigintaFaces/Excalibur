2. **[CONTINUE] PM single-filer for in-thread findings** (new rule, applied mid-S841 after the 2nd race).
   Evidence: the mutual-dedup race fired twice; single-filer + announce-the-survivor eliminated it. Keep it.

   Evidence: recurring flush refusal forced 3 standalone reconcile commits. The DB-touch workaround is proven; mechanize it in the integration-commit path.

4. **[CONTINUE] STRATEGY for class-extinction sprints + structural-enforcement-first + author≠implementer locks.**

5. **[TRY] A liveness pre-check before each phase advance** (whois the next-phase owner) so a terminated owner is caught at dispatch, not discovered when its phase stalls.
