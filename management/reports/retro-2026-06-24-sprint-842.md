## Recommendations (ordered by impact)

1. **[CHANGE — escalate] Mechanize the F-5 cross-project sweep NOW; it has recurred 3 sprints.**
   For any type-contract change, a mandatory IMPLEMENT step: `grep -rl <TypeName\|contract-string>` across the **entire test tree** (all projects), triage every hit before locks-done. The repeated manual rec isn't sticking — make it a checklist gate in `run-current-tasks`, or a pre-REVIEW script. 2. **[TRY] Before dispatching a fix for a REVIEW/DOCS-found issue, check for reviewer `[AUTO-FIX]`.**
   New near-miss this sprint (Backend dispatched onto SA's concurrent auto-fix). For AUTO-FIX-class items (stale docs, mechanical), the reviewer often takes it; confirm ownership before assigning a separate implementer. Composes with coordinate-before-parallel-work.

3. **[CHANGE] Finally mechanize the bd-flush DB-touch in the integration-commit path.**
   3rd sprint of standalone reconcile commits (3 this sprint). The DB-touch-then-flush workaround is proven; bake it into the commit helper per the standing `bd-flush-then-commit` rule. Measurement: 0 standalone reconcile commits next multi-worker sprint.

4. **[CONTINUE] The liveness pre-check (whois next-phase owner) + the mtime-gate.**
   The S841 rec applied this sprint caught a dead REVIEW_CODE owner at dispatch and the mtime-gate correctly *held* on a live-but-frozen SA. Both worked. Keep; push for server-side liveness-on-dispatch as the structural fix.

5. **[CONTINUE] Capability-by-composition + structural-enforcement-first + author≠implementer locks + STRATEGY gate.**
