## What the sprint actually was

Planned as backlog clearance (~150 beads, 9 lanes). It became an **instrument audit**, because the
first blocker found was that committed mainline had not compiled for two days while every gate
reported green. Everything after that followed from asking why.

## Five instruments reporting health they did not have

1. **Build gates compiled the working tree, not the committed SHA.** Two days of green on a
   non-compiling mainline.
2. **The shipped-DDL sweep cannot see bracketed `[Schema].[Table]`.** No PASS, no REFUSE, no signal —
   a blind spot reports clean, where a coverage gap at least refuses.
3. **`docs-site` build: corrupt install, and `npm run build` exits 0 when the binary is absent.** A
   gate that passes when it cannot run.
4. **Three retired guards still invoked**, wrapped in `if [ -f ]`, so *cannot-run* silently became
   *found-nothing*.
5. **`.dts/` — the discovery log, our "durable" record — is gitignored.** A push replicates only what
   git tracks, and nobody had checked whether the durable-looking artifact was durable.

## The dominant failure mode was NOT bad reasoning

Roughly forty self-corrections were logged. Nearly every significant finding arrived as someone
withdrawing a claim they had made minutes earlier. **The rules were right; the inputs fed to them were
unverified.** Eight of the night's errors were a search whose *scope* was narrower than the *claim* it
supported:

| who | the narrow scope | what was claimed |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | guard *names* via `grep -c` | "the guards are present" |
| integrator | guard *loop list* only | "the guards cannot fire" |
| integrator | `.md` glob, no `.cs` | "**exhaustive** sweep" |
| integrator | `///` doc comments only | corrected sweep — missed exception strings |
| ProductManager | hand-enumerated surfaces ×3 | "the surface list" |
| ProjectReviewer | verb-triggers (*"easy to grep"*) | "the parity gate" |
| DocumentationWriter | 4 directories | "the gate is unwired" |
| integrator | artifact *quality* not *tracked status* | "this is polish, decline it" |

**Every one was caught by another agent re-measuring, never by a gate.** The terminating fix came from
BackendDeveloper: **search the claim sentence, not the topic keyword** — 368 candidate files → 5 real
sites.

The night's sharpest single artifact is the CEO's own correction:

> *"I wrote 'confirmed independently from the same source.' Read that sentence back — it refutes
> itself. Reading the same output twice is not independent verification. It is one measurement,
> performed twice."*

That sentence describes every instrument failure above.

## Also learned: timing is part of a measurement

**Four crossed-message pairs**, each with both parties correct about *different instants* — a stale
export read, a preservation inversion, a root-cause retraction landing as it was endorsed, and a carry
ruling reported as overridden when it had been adopted. In a mesh this fast, **when** a reading was
taken is part of the reading, and we have no convention for stamping it.

control*; the **scope-of-the-search** facet was uncovered. Added there as a clause.
