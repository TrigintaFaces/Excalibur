  is a catalogue: live-but-uncommitted hooks (worked, so nothing pressured a commit), folklore in comments
  (the "~2h restart" that has zero vendor basis), mis-citations of hashes/counts/scope, a false "39-blank-lines"
  mechanism three agents independently invented, an audit whose own false claim was in git and its correction
  wasn't. Every one was caught — but each cost cycles.
- **Post-phase coupled commit.** Landing B1+B2+F1 *after* REVIEW_CODE/REVIEW_ARCH forced an honest re-verify at
  the new HEAD (extra reviewer round). `forge-integration-conventions` clause 2 (commit additive deltas BEFORE
  REVIEW) is the standing fix; the coupling settled late under the shared-tree gate-wiring race.
- **AV throttle** made commits ~1hr and gate self-tests 90–280s — the environmental tax under which every honesty
  failure compounded.

   discipline, not a new rule, is the fix.
