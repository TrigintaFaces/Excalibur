## What Worked Well

- **Layered review caught what the test suite structurally couldn't — twice.** REVIEW_CODE found egwtku **inert** (wired-nowhere); REVIEW_ARCH found it **serializer-inert** (STJ-only attrs under the default Newtonsoft client). The TEST 10-shard was GREEN through both — unit mocks never exercised the real serializer/wiring seam. The multi-phase pipeline is earning its cost.
- **The coordination board ended the gate-2 churn.** After Platform (returning from offline) built the SA-**rejected** `IHostedService` mechanism on stale context — caught 4× pre-integration (PM/PdM/SA/SENTINEL) — a single authoritative board (re-pull-before-build/claim, ready-signal-cites-the-ruling, one-file-one-owner) converged the whole team and the re-do landed clean on the first verified attempt.
- **Implementer real-extent check prevented a half-fix.** Platform's whole-document check found Facet 2 (PK `subscriptionId`) before editing — fixing only `id` would have shipped half-broken.
- **Honest accounting held under pressure.** FR-B1 reliability was *never* reported FULL while inert; the premature "egwtku FULL" was reconciled by annotation; SPEC-owner re-confirmed on committed HEAD. No false-green reached a changelog.
- **Evidence over authority — three grounded self-corrections.** PM clause-5 (transitive), SA clause-5 (same), SA evttu3 (fail-closed is correct) — each a fast, friendly retraction on run→read→cite.

## What Didn't Work

- **Stale-context after a worker returns from offline = the highest-cost failure mode.** Platform built a 4×-rejected mechanism because it acted on its own pre-ruling message without re-pulling. Cost: a full build + 4 reviewer catches + a rebuild. → the board's re-pull rule is the fix; promote it.
- **The serializer-honoring gap slipped two gates.** Tests' facet-3 lock asserted *attribute-presence* (STJ-only), and the real-Cosmos lock used an **STJ-configured** client — so both were vacuous on the exact default-Newtonsoft bug. Only REVIEW_ARCH source-reasoning caught it.
- **Tracker stale-snapshot bit once.** `bd sync --flush-only` no-op'd → the first close commit captured a pre-close snapshot; caught by my own forge-clause-6 verify, fixed via `bd export`. The export-not-flush path should be the default for close commits.

## Surprises

- The keystone's real extent was **2 layers deeper** than spec'd (inert → serializer-inert), and the serializer layer was invisible to ~73k passing tests because the framework hands serialization to the **consumer-supplied** Cosmos Container (SDK-v3 default = Newtonsoft, not STJ).
- A wrong "fix" was nearly scheduled: the REVIEW_ARCH `evttu3` "SaveAsync should fail-open" MED would have converted a **correct** durability fail-closed into a data-loss bug — caught by PdM/PM SPEC reasoning before it was carved.

## Recommendations (ordered by impact)

1. **[CONTINUE] Keep the multi-phase review with source-reasoning at REVIEW_ARCH.**
   Evidence: it caught 2 BLOCKING `advertised-but-inert` defects that ~73k GREEN tests + a 6-point REVIEW_CODE missed. The architectural lens ("framework doesn't own the serializer") is irreplaceable.

2. **[CHANGE] Real-infra & serializer locks must bind the SEMANTIC under the DEFAULT client, not attribute-presence under a hand-configured one.**
   Evidence: facet-3 asserted the STJ attr; the jattxa lock used an STJ client — both vacuous on the default-Newtonsoft bug. Lock the *emitted JSON keys* under the SDK-default serializer + the resume-at-persisted-position behavior. Effort: low (test-authoring discipline). *(promote to a rule via /self-improving.)*

3. **[CHANGE] Make `bd export --no-auto-import` the default for close/close-out commits, not `bd sync --flush-only`.**
   Evidence: flush-only no-op'd and shipped a stale pre-close tracker snapshot (caught by forge-clause-6). Export is authoritative. Effort: trivial (already in bd-flush-then-commit clause 6; enforce at the close-commit step).

4. **[TRY] On a worker returning from offline, require an explicit "re-pulled @ <HEAD>, reconciled to ruling <msgid>" line before its first edit/claim.**
   Hypothesis: kills the stale-context double-fault (the sprint's single most expensive miss). Measurement: zero rejected-mechanism rebuilds next sprint. *(The coordination board's rule #1, made a mechanical pre-edit checkpoint.)*

5. **[CONTINUE] Honest accounting on completing-fix traceability.**
   Evidence: annotate-not-reopen + "not FULL until green on committed HEAD" + SPEC-owner re-confirm prevented a false-green changelog. Keep the close-owner reconciliation as a standing CLOSE step.
