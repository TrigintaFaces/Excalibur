## Recommendations (ordered by impact)

1. **[CHANGE] Dedup closes must verify the canonical's *delivered (committed)* scope covers the dup's
   deliverable — a comment-port / "will be ported" is not a deliverable.** Evidence: voxr6n closed-but-
   incomplete; the SagaOptions leak reached committed HEAD `a56eaf922`. Effort: low (a check at dedup-close).
   → Promote to a rule via `/self-improving` (extends `verify-before-claiming` + `forge-integration-conventions`
   clause 6 to the dedup-close case).
2. **[CHANGE] Run the ShippingOnly-parity (sync-ci-cd) check at project-ADD time, not just CLOSE.** Evidence:
   f338mp missing from `ShippingOnly.slnf` blocked the CLOSE gate. Effort: low — add the slnf-parity assert to
   the new-project pre-commit/`sync-ci-cd` step so it fails when the keystone lands, not 9 phases later.
3. **[CONTINUE] Coupled-set integration with explicit impl clean-rebuild + lock-gate + committed-HEAD verify
   before commit.** Evidence: zero bad commits across 14 integration commits; 9/9 honest-log lock caught any
   template/assertion drift mechanically.
4. **[CONTINUE] Full-surface enumeration at REVIEW_ARCH (not keystone-scoped).** Evidence: SagaOptions gap
   found there, after REVIEW_CODE's keystone passes missed it — the recurring F-5/full-surface theme.
5. **[TRY] Treat a red AOT gate with 0 IL warnings as an environment waiver, fast.** Hypothesis: separating
   "compat (IL warnings)" from "native-link (toolchain)" in the gate's verdict avoids re-litigating an
   environmental failure each sprint. Measurement: the AOT gate reports compat-PASS / link-ENV-SKIP distinctly.
