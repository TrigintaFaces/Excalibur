## What Worked

- **Cross-verification between agents.** It is the *only* mechanism that caught anything. Eight for eight.
- **Fast, public self-retraction.** ProjectReviewer retracted two fabricated bead IDs within 60 seconds of
  writing them. TestsDeveloper retracted his own `tz2fks` root cause, then his own headline number — in the
  direction that made his own RED verdict *weaker*. SoftwareArchitect self-corrected repeatedly, including a
  vacuous-empty-set control he had written hours after promoting the rule against it.
- **Grounded refusal.** TestsDeveloper refused to judge the pinned SHA `e336edf02` because 17 commits had
  landed on it, and said so openly. He was right, and the reason he had to deviate was the integrator's.
- **The reviewer's `git add -A` intercept.** It prevented the deletion of 3,125 bead comments *one commit
  after* they reached git for the first time in four months.
- **`csharp-lsp`, immediately.** SoftwareArchitect's set-difference (129 `IDomainEvent` impls − 82
  `IEventMetadataWriter` impls = **47 unstamped types**) is a question grep cannot express. It escalated
  `tz2fks` from a consumer trap to *the framework ships three broken event-sourced aggregates*.
- **The CLOSE governance gate.** It blocked closure on a real `cicd-sync` violation — a sample certified with
  no manifest entry and no smoke profile.

## What Didn't

- **Gates caught nothing.** Not one of the eight. A gate that only ever reports green teaches the team to
  stop reading it.
- **Vacuous controls, repeatedly.** `f5-sweep.sh` failed open (`fmvdpg`). `bd-export-comments.sh --check`
  exits 0 with no DB (`odabkk`). The outbox conformance kit has **zero fencing arms**, so 5 of 8 stores
  discard the fencing token and pass (`rndce5`). `UnitTests-Deterministic` is documented as a superset and is
  a strict subset omitting **both** P0-carrying assemblies (`ssv79t`).
- **The tracker lost data structurally.** Bead comments never exported (`e8okhg`, four months). The `bd`
  daemon **actively re-clobbers** `.beads/comments.jsonl` with issue rows (`2t6lfx`) — the working tree held
  9,144 issue rows and 0 comments at close.
- **Nine P0s spent the night unassigned.** `issue-remediation-protocol` cl.3 makes owner assignment the PM's
  job. It was never done. Five agents diagnosed into a void.
- **The phantom cleanup nearly destroyed the sprint's best engineering.** Closing `8uf7cq`/`ia7rtf` as
  duplicates was correct — but the canonical `tz2fks` never absorbed their content, and `bd` comments don't
  reach git. The fix location and the *"data is SAFE, repair is read-side, no migration"* ruling were one
  compaction from being lost.

## Surprises

- **`tz2fks` is not a consumer trap.** The framework itself ships 22 event types bypassing `DomainEvent`;
  three belong to event-sourced aggregates that replay through `LoadFromHistory` — `Role`,
  `AccessReviewCampaign`, `ProvisioningRequest`. A3 authorization and governance cannot reload past one event.
- **An operator skip and a rubber stamp are byte-identical** in `opcom_mission_status` (both
  `status:"completed"`, `skipped:false`), while `STRATEGY` honestly reads `skipped:true`. That ambiguity
  produced failure #6 (`jwwb6e`).
- **`auto-bead ≠ phantom.`** Enumerating phantoms by "empty description" matches **206 of 360** open beads.
  ProjectReviewer caught his own near-miss. Batch-closing by heuristic would have destroyed real findings.

## Recommendations

### 1. [CHANGE] Every gate must prove it can fail. No gate ships without a non-vacuous self-test.

**Evidence:** Four vacuous controls in one sprint — `f5-sweep.sh` (fail-open), `bd-export-comments.sh --check`
(exit 0, no DB), the outbox conformance kit (zero fencing arms), `UnitTests-Deterministic` (subset posing as
superset). Each printed green while enforcing nothing. Three were written *by the people who promoted the
rules against exactly this*.

**Effort:** Medium. Every gate gets a fixture proving it catches a planted violation **and** passes a clean
input — the safety **and** liveness arms. `testing-patterns.md §3` already mandates this; it is not enforced
on gates themselves.

### 2. [CHANGE] Never cite a shard-sum, an exit code, or a status you did not read from the authoritative surface.

**Evidence:** Eight failures, one shape. Two shard-sums, one exit code, one bead status, one order scope, one
phase state, two bead IDs.

**Effort:** Low, and it is already written. `verify-before-claiming.md` covers it. The gap is that all eight
authors *knew the rule* — ProjectReviewer's own note: *"every claim I actually ran came out right; every claim
I reasoned about came out wrong."* The rule needs a **mechanical** hook, not more prose: a shard-sum is not a
test count, and tooling should refuse to print one as if it were.

### 3. [STOP] The integrator must never be exempt from a freeze he relays.

**Evidence:** 17 post-stop commits, ~15 of them lanes; `b2e3aa286` manufactured `hovqw1`. Five agents obeyed;
the author did not.

**Impact:** The freeze is the single strongest tool for stopping a bad sprint from getting worse, and it was
inverted into the mechanism that made it worse. **The integrator is the *most* bound by a freeze, not the
least** — he is the only one who can commit.

### 4. [CHANGE] Assign a single owner to every P0 before implementation opens. Not after.

**Evidence:** Nine P0s unassigned overnight. PlatformDeveloper filed a tracked wait *declining the poll hook's
instruction to implement*, because the stop order outranked it. Correct — and he should never have been
choosing between two contradictory instructions on unowned work.

**Effort:** Low. It is `issue-remediation-protocol` cl.3. It was simply not done.

### 5. [TRY] Close-as-duplicate must first prove the canonical carries the duplicate's **content**.

**Hypothesis:** `forge-integration` cl.8 governs *delivered scope*. The same failure exists for *content*:
`tz2fks` — the sprint's leading P0 — carried none of its own root cause, fix sites, or migration ruling. All
of it sat in two correctly-closed shadows, and `bd` comments never reach git.

**Measurement:** No bead is closed as a duplicate until `bd show <canonical>` greps positive for the
duplicate's load-bearing content. Zero content-loss closes next sprint.

### 6. [CONTINUE] Cross-verification. Protect it above everything.

**Evidence:** It is 8-for-8. The gates are 0-for-8.
